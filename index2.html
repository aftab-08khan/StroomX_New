What we do?

We collaborate with Franchisees around the world and provide them with all necessary resources that enable them to transform traditional centers into an engaging workshops, equipped with the latest Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics (STEM) tools to help students acquire the 21st century skills.

Curriculum

Our franchisees have access to hundreds of model builds and resources that progress from early learning to robotics and coding consisting of numerous activities to engage children with, from After-school classes, field trips, workshops, birthday parties and the all-time favorite themed Camps during the year!

Marketing

Marketing programs streamline efficiencies for all corporate marketing initiatives and for our franchisees with support, through guidance, knowledge, training, and continuing education necessary to reach goals in order to continue to build sustainable, successful businesses.

Technology

We have developed our own Franchise Management System (FMS) to serve our franchisees better. Our FMS web application streamlines the booking process, payments and management of the Bricks 4 Kidz® business for our franchisees.
